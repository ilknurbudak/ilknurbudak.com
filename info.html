Faces, masks and hidden figures, drawn in ink and charcoal.

about

İlknur Budak is an artist and illustrator working since 2019 across drawing, moving image and freehand tattooing. Her drawings return to silence, withdrawal and the inner life of the self: figures that hide inside heads, faces that are covered, sunk or turned away.

experience

  • since 2023Illustrator. Independent practice in drawing, moving image and tattoo design
  • since 2021Tattoo artist. Freehand tattooing; worked with various studios across several cities, now working independently
  • since 2020Painter. Acrylic and oil painting in Istanbul; worked with various galleries, now working independently
  • 2019Edirnekâri. Practice in the traditional decorative painting of Edirne

education

Left formal fine arts studies to build her own practice; self-taught ever since.
